# Evani.Materials: batch 4 build plan (people and core clothing)

**In plain words, for Chris.** Batch 4 adds **120 materials for people and their clothes**: 26 skin tones, 17 skin traits (stubble, scars, bruises, tattoos, freckled sun damage, lips, make-up), 24 hair and beard looks, 10 eyes, teeth and nails, 27 fabrics (checks, tartans, tweeds, denims, felts) and 11 leathers. 3 already work. **56 more can be set right now** on what the engine already draws (skin tones, plain hair colours, most eyes, plain leathers). The rest needs four pieces: **woven checks and stripes** for cloth (gingham, tartan, houndstooth, pinstripe all come from one "coloured yarns" switch), **a few more eye details** (gold flecks, a milky film, a glass eye, and horse and cat pupils for the animal batches), **two leather looks** (pressed snake-scale and old crazed leather), and the big one, **skin and hair that take rain, mud, dust, snow and blood** like every other material, which needs a short **research step first**. The work is **15 packages and one research step**; three packages and the research can start today, side by side.

Status: design only (2026-10-25). Nothing here is built. Builders take one package at a time.

Inputs read for this plan: `CATALOGUE_PLAN.md`, `catalogue.csv` and BATCH2/BATCH3 plans (branch `material-catalogue`); on `matcat-engine` (read only): `DECISIONS.md` (batch 1 design to the tier-switch fix), `BATCH1_BUILD_PLAN.md` (P15 verdicts), `crates/evani-material-recipes` (22 recipe-stage recipes, 8 layers), `crates/evani-material-character` (`record.rs`, `character.wgsl`), `catalog/materials/*.json`; on `b3-patterns` (read only): `DECISIONS.md` B3-P1 to the size trim (layouts, cut-out, see-through pass, glass, thin and cloth recipe stages, wood layouts part); the research reports in `Evani.Dev/.vibedeck/research/` (2026-10-22 skin, eye, hair and cloth; 2026-10-24 fur and hide).

### 3.4 New capability, research first: skin and hair through the recipe stage (B4-R1, then B4-P4 to P6)

Today skin, hair and eye are drawn only by the character family. Batch 1's composition rule is "a character kind never carries a recipe block", so **no condition layer reaches skin or hair** (batch 1 section 7 filed this for batch 4), and the family has no per-pixel traits (stubble, bruise, tattoo, vitiligo, chapped lips, braids, coils, salt-and-pepper mix). 44 skin and 24 hair rows list wet, mud, dust, snow and blood.

**The likely design** (to be confirmed by B4-R1, not built before): lift the rule for skin and hair only. The recipe stage runs first (pattern, traits, conditions) and hands base colour, normal, roughness and occlusion to the family's shading (the pre-integrated skin scatter, the two-lobe hair highlight and its soft edge coverage). Batch 3 already found that the patches stack (the character patch returns a coverage struct, and the blend wrap takes it).

**Research questions B4-R1 must answer** (documents and a throwaway spike on a scratch branch only; written up in `.vibedeck/research/` and as a DECISIONS proposal):

1. **Order and anchors.** Can one material carry `evani_kind` skin or hair **and** `extras.evani_recipe`, with `mat_surface` running before `char_shade`? Which anchor lines exist in all four texts (plain, layered, char, char+layers), with the cut-out and blend patches on and off? Does naga's uniformity analysis pass with `char_frame` and `mat_frame` both before the discard?
2. **What the family should take from the recipe.** Albedo surely; the normal (the family adds its own pores: double normal edits, or the recipe's pores replace the family's?); roughness; the scatter colour. What does the family ignore so a blood or mud pixel is not lit as glowing skin (a coverage or "plain" weight from the condition layers that switches the scatter off under mud and blood)?
3. **Wet skin and wet hair.** Is the wet layer's darken-and-gloss right for skin (it should add a sharp lobe, not darken much) and hair (clumped strands, darker, glossier)? A per-recipe wet response, or the layer as is?
4. **Hair coverage.** The family writes the hair's soft edge as a sample mask; batch 3's cut-out uses alpha MASK with a 2 × 2 screen door. Can braids, coils and sparse body hair feed the family's coverage without the two fighting? Do eyelashes and body hair need the cut-out?
5. **The alternative.** A standalone recipe-stage skin and hair (wrap scatter like `hide`, a Kajiya-Kay rim like `fur`), no family at all. Bytes (the family is about 13.6 KB gzip; a recipe about 1.5 KB each) against the close-up look. Chris's rule is the same look on every device, so the answer cannot be "family on PC, standalone on phones".
6. **Pipeline build time.** The style step taught that a second recipe call site multiplies the browser's compile time (32 s to 374 s). With the character, recipe, cut-out, blend and style patches all on, is there still exactly one recipe call? What does the viewer's pipeline build cost with all of it?
7. **The tier-switch rule.** Nothing may write a tier or a slider into a shader slot (2026-10-25 freeze). Confirm the composition reads only bindings 12 to 14.
8. **Face placement.** Rosy cheeks, kohl round the eyes, a pale-ill shadow under the eyes, stubble on the jaw: drawn by the material over the whole part it is assigned to, or placed with batch 5's region mask (B5-R1) on a head? Recommend which rows are fine as whole-material looks.
9. **Prior art, ideas only:** Filament's subsurface and cloth models, Bevy's extended-material order, the 2026-10-22 skin, eye and hair report's sources; code written fresh, licences noted.

**Then:** B4-P4 builds the composition plumbing (engine, 16-file proof), and B4-P5 (skin) and B4-P6 (hair) build the two recipe stages in parallel.

- **skin (code 16) recipe stage**, `backend: recipes`, `also: ["character"]`. Variants tone 0, trait 1 (existing) + lips 2, makeup 3, mucosa 4 (gums), stubble 5, scar 6, burn 7, bruise 8, tattoo 9 (batch 3's layout motif, softened under the skin), patches 10 (vitiligo), callus 11. Numbers keep their six slots + `amount`, `trait_scale`, `wet` (lip gloss, gums), `edge` (make-up smudge or chalky edge). Colour C is the trait colour (ink, bruise, paint, kohl). Group `skin`. Budget ≤ 1.5 KB.
- **hair (code 17) recipe stage**, `backend: recipes`, `also: ["character"]`. Variants straight, curly, coarse (existing) + coily 3, braided 4, sparse 5 (eyebrow, lash, body hair: colour C, the skin, shows between hairs). Numbers keep their four slots + `mix` (salt-and-pepper share of colour C strands), `fade` (lighter toward the tip: pastel dye), `curl_scale`, `gloss` (waxed). Group `fur`. Budget ≤ 1.5 KB.
