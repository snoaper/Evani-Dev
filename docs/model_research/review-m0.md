# Evani.Model M0: independent review

Reviewer: QQ (Opus, extra-high effort), 2026-10-15, on branch `evani-model` (6 commits on dfc9ae3). I did not write the M0 work. Scope: the brief (docs/EVANI_MODEL_BRIEF.md), the 20 component notes, the known / ours / why / result table, plan-baselines.md, plan-m1.md, plan-decisions.md, docs/EVANI_MODEL.md, docs/MODEL_API.md, tests/model_bench/ and the DECISIONS.md entry.

## Verdict: approve with fixes

No blockers. The research is substantive and specific, every cited paper I checked says what the notes claim, the clean-room record is honest, the licence statements are right, the 30 intents parse and match their manifest, nothing beyond M0 was built and `cargo xtask check` is clean. Five major issues should be fixed in the documents before Chris signs the decision list, because two of them change what he is being asked to decide (D5, and a dependency the plan assumes without asking). All five are document work (about half a day); none needs a redesign of the approach.

What is strong: the "concepts in, maths decides" split is carried through every note; the closed vocabulary, field-based parts, surface-aware contact (which closes CADSmith's joint-gap failure by construction), LODs by meaning and collision from the part graph are real improvements, not restatements; the kart reference capture is exact about the kit's code; the decision list is honest about what this machine cannot test.

## Findings

### Blockers
None.

### Major

**M1. The determinism design does not deliver the guarantee it claims.**
Evidence: note 07 line 19, "a candidate is only dropped when its score is below the cut by more than a measured GPU-error margin ... So the same survivors and the same final model come out on every machine for the same effort"; note 07 line 20 (early stop decided on the rung's top K); note 08 line 18, refinement budget is "whatever the search left of the call's time budget"; note 18 line 18, "the rung sizes, resolutions and refinement population are computed from the measured throughput"; note 18 line 21, "margin-safe pruning ... so GPU rounding cannot change who survives"; D5 (plan-decisions.md line 11) fixes only the candidate count.
Why it is wrong: (a) the cut itself is computed from GPU scores, so it moves between vendors; a candidate inside the margin on one machine is outside it on another, the survivor sets differ, and the CPU re-scores different finalists. (b) A margin "measured" on one adapter does not bound another vendor's error. (c) Early stop is decided on GPU scores. (d) Rung sizes, resolutions and the refinement budget follow the clock and measured throughput, so even `explore: 256` on two machines of the same tier can run different schedules. With (d), test H's "identical model hashes everywhere" fails even at a fixed effort.
Fix (document now, build in M2): define the resolved effort as the whole schedule (candidates per rung, rung resolutions, refinement population and generations, early-stop rule), chosen once before the search from the start-up calibration and recorded; nothing during the run reads the clock. Make every keep / drop decision deterministic: the GPU returns each score as an interval (for example from the count of voxels whose field value is within a bound of zero, the bound checked per adapter in the hardware matrix); candidates whose interval straddles the cut are re-scored on the CPU at that rung, and early stop and finalists use CPU scores only. Reword D5 to promise "same intent, seed and resolved effort (the full schedule) give the same hash", and say plainly that `explore: auto` gives the same result only on machines that resolve to the same schedule.

**M2. A dependency the plan relies on is not on the decision list: geocheck's mesh validation pulls in evani-core and evani-render.**
Evidence: docs/EVANI_MODEL.md line 21, "the library never depends on evani-core or evani-render ... It may use geocheck's mesh validation library (our own code)"; note 13 line 19 and note 20 line 10 use `meshcheck::validate` inside compile; plan-m1 T9 depends on it; tools/evani-geocheck/Cargo.toml lines 25 to 27 depend on evani-core, evani-render and serde_json unconditionally (even with default features off).
Why it matters: it breaks the "never depends on evani-core or evani-render" rule and brings their whole dependency tree into evani-model, against the brief's dependency rule, without asking. Also, GEOCHECK.md line 158 says its self-intersection test is O(n²) with a box prefilter: inside a kart compile with a 60k-triangle Ultra tier and a 10 s budget this must be measured.
Fix: add a decision (D17 below). Recommended: move `meshcheck` into a tiny crate with no engine dependencies that geocheck re-exports (a small change to a main-line tool, so Chris should agree), and give it a spatial-grid self-intersection test.

**M3. Nothing designs how Evani.Model makes pictures, and M4's proof needs engine work that is not filed.**
Evidence: MODEL_API.md line 179 (`model_sheet`: blueprint, raking light, variants, curvature, thickness), note 02 (calibration contact sheets), note 11 (the critic's one sheet), brief test K (contact sheets for Chris): no note designs the renderer behind them. Note 12 lines 20 to 23 send medium relief to normal maps and fine relief to "the look's shader"; the engine has no normal-map, tangent or micro-detail support today (no match in crates/evani-render/src or looks/*/src). D11 (plan-decisions.md line 17) says only M5 waits for Stage 3.
Why it matters: M3 calibration and the critic need sheets; M4's done line ("tread, bolts and panel lines correct on every tier") cannot be checked in the game until glTF import (Stage 3) and normal-map and micro-detail shading exist, which is engine and look work this track may not do itself.
Fix: add a component note 21 "sheets and previews" (our own sphere tracer of the field on the CPU and GPU, toon bands read from the look's registered params, our PNG writer, the five sheet kinds) with a known / ours / why / result row; extend D11 so M4's in-game proof also waits for Stage 3, with normal maps and a micro-detail hook in the toon look filed in CORE_REQUESTS.md, and M4 judged in Evani.Model's own preview until then (D19 below).

**M4. M0's deliverable is only partly there, and there is no M0 report against the tests.**
Evidence: brief line 149, "M0 Research: clean-room notes and the known/ours/why table for every component, plus the benchmark set and baselines"; brief line 137, "30 intents with references"; brief line 148, each milestone ends with "a report against the relevant tests"; brief line 20, a licence and code-similarity scan before each sign-off. The references are plans (tests/model_bench/README.md: "Reference files ... are made in M1"), the baselines are a plan (plan-baselines.md), and no M0 scan record exists (plan-m1.md's scan is "run before every milestone sign-off").
Why it matters: deferring both is sensible (references need code; baseline F needs a harness and a token budget), but it is implied by D9 and D12, never put to Chris as "M0 closes without these".
Fix: add a short M0 report section to docs/model_research/README.md: test B (30 intents done, references in M1 task T2), test F (deferred to M5, D9), test I (notes and table complete for 20 components; only .md, .json and .evmodel files added; no third-party files; this review's citation and wording spot-check). Add decision D16 below.

**M5. Two measurements as planned cannot show the brief's efficiency targets.**
(a) Priors (test D). Evidence: note 09 line 18 and tests/model_bench/README.md line 14 rely on approvals growing across the run order, but nobody approves during an automated run, and 7 of the 8 karts say `like: bench/kart_01_pip`, so "fewer candidates over time" would mostly measure `like`, not priors. Fix: a benchmark-only acceptance rule (D18 below) writing to a benchmark-only prior file; keep `like` identical in both arms of the priors ablation; report two run orders.
(b) Baseline F. Evidence: plan-baselines.md line 15 caps each baseline run at 12 calls and 30 minutes. With Evani.Model's median of 2 calls the cap limits the measurable gap to 6x, and a capped run that stops below the quality bar makes "at equal or better quality" meaningless. Fix: measure calls and tokens to reach a fixed bar (all musts pass and IoU 0.9) with a generous cap (about 40 calls or 90 minutes), and report the share of runs that never reach it.

### Minor

1. **Estimates look optimistic** (plan-m1.md line 34: 25 to 35 agent-days). T9 (adaptive octree dual contouring with multi-patch cells, crease hints and a re-extraction loop) is "XL, 4 days or more"; T5 also carries subdivision cages with a BVH and winding numbers; T11 includes our own sparse conformal solver and MikkTSpace from its description. Expect 40 to 60 agent-days, or cut M1 to what the kart needs (cages, lofts, trees, blended bodies and the conformal fallback after the M1 gate). T12 also needs a second pass after T11 (tangents, UVs, maps).
2. **Note 13 line 19 overstates "No self-intersections".** Clamping each vertex to its cell does not stop neighbouring quads from crossing (Ju and Udeshi 2006 needed more than that); the validator with local re-extraction is the real guarantee. Say so and budget its cost.
3. **Pro tier threshold units.** nvidia-smi reports an A10G (the planned Pro test machine, g5) at about 22.5 GiB usable and an RTX 4090 just under 24 GiB, so "24 GB or more" read in GiB would class both as Standard. Define it (for example "at least 22 GiB reported"), and say whether Apple Silicon with 24 GB or more unified memory is Pro (memory rule) or Standard (the brief's wording).
4. **"tier" means two things.** MODEL_API uses `tier` for the compute tier (line 136, `"lite"`) and `tier` / `tiers` for render tiers (`model_check` line 174, `model_compile` line 183). Name the compute one `compute` (or `hw`) so agents cannot mix them.
5. **The gate IoU's resolution is undefined.** References are 512 px masks; scoring rungs are 32 to 128 cells. State that the reported and gate IoU is the CPU score at 512 px against the reference mask.
6. **Frame centre undefined.** manifest.json line 15 says "(frame_m, centre)" but no item gives a centre. Define the rule (for example footprint centre in x and z, ground at the frame's bottom; floating items centred).
7. **Reference descriptions that contradict their own sizes** (for task T2): cloud (front 2.0 m wide and 1.6 m tall, but six spheres of at most 1.5 m in a line, cut flat, cannot reach that); candy cane (shaft to 2.1 m plus centre-line radius 0.3 plus tube radius 0.15 puts the top at 2.55 m, not 2.5; the hook end reaches 1.6 m, not 1.7); rock (the first ellipsoid, 1.4 m in x, is wider than the stated 1.2 m front width); cactus ("out 0.4 m" measured from the trunk axis or its surface gives 1.05 or 1.65 m width against a 1.4 m box). The reference tool should assert every assembly fits its fit_box and frame.
8. **`seats: bench/chibi_body`** in all 8 kart intents (for example kart_01_pip.evmodel line 22) points at the standing chibi benchmark body, while manifest.json line 166 and the README say the seated driver taken from the kart (instances 15 to 21) is what the `seats` must uses. Evani.Model has no posing, so a standing body cannot be seated. Give the seated driver its own ref (for example `bench/kart_driver`) and point the karts at it.
9. **Under D4 option A** the rival intents' adjectives (Rumble's `very_oversized` tyres and 4 exhausts, Clover's removed wing) would fight the shared base reference; the README only says their references change. Under A the rival intents must be reduced to the base too.
10. **`fit_box` semantics.** MODEL_API 1.6 says "constraint (scale or clamp), then check": inside the box, or matching it within a tolerance? kart_01's box equals its measured size while the cactus's depth (1.0 m) is far larger than its 0.6 m trunk. State "the model's box must lie inside; scaling only when asked".
11. **Reading image references** (MODEL_API line 98: "images thresholded to masks") needs a full PNG inflate decoder, and other formats more; the plan only writes stored-block PNGs. Either accept only our own masks until a decoder is planned (our own inflate, no new crate), or list a dependency for Chris. This can be decided without Chris: restrict to our own masks for M1 and M2.
12. **The differentiable-rendering alternative was substituted.** The brief asks to evaluate "a differentiable-rendering fit"; note 08 line 20 proposes its own analytic boundary-gradient fit instead. Reasonable and probably better, but the brief says to flag such changes: add it to the README's "contradicts or stretches the brief" list.
13. **Determinism checklist gaps** (note 18): also forbid iterating randomised hash maps and collecting parallel results unordered on the compile path.
14. **Note 12:** shader micro-detail below about 1.5 pixels must be band-limited in the shader (fade by screen-space derivatives) or it shimmers. "Dirt" in the wear rules is colour, outside the brief.
15. **Benchmark coverage:** no intent uses the `clear`, `min_size` or `max_size` must kinds or the `weave` pattern (dents and scratches appear only through `wear`), and the 180-edit set for test C (note 10) is not in tests/model_bench. Add both before M3.
16. **The research record** (README line 6) lists no reading of docs/KIT_PROCESS.md or catalog/genres/, which the brief names (line 110). Add them or say why they are not needed.
17. **Note 09:** per-dimension inverse-CDF warping implies a diagonal covariance per mixture component; say so.
18. **Note 20:** a recipe is "a few hundred bytes", but compiling it in the player's browser also needs the compiler, templates and style data in the download; say so when recipe delivery is designed.
