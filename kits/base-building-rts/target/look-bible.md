# Look bible: Cinder Ridge

Stylized PBR (art pass, K5b): vibrant, chunky, clean materials. Strategy clarity first: every team colour, unit role and resource reads from the default camera height at phone size.

## Palette (sRGB hex)

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| #1fb5a8 | Player team colour (roofs, unit bands, selection rings) |
| #e0662b | Rival team colour (roofs, unit bands) |

| #6f8f4e | Highland grass (ground) |

## Shape language

- Buildings are chunky blocks on wide bevelled foundations, roof in team colour, footprint snapped to the 2 m grid.
- Units are short and wide (a worker is a squat capsule with a crate on its back; a trooper a tall narrow capsule; a rumbler a low hull with a turret dome), so roles read by silhouette at 30 px.
- Bevels everywhere, no thin spikes; proportions about 1.3x chunkier than real.

## Sky and backdrop

The camera looks down, so the sky shows only at the top of a tilted view: a pale morning blue with light haze. The map is ringed by basalt ridges and pine slopes that fade into haze, so no camera sees the world edge.
