# HUD layout

The HUD is light DOM (`apps/match-3/index.html`, filled by `src/ui.rs`) placed every frame from the kit's pure layout (`kits/evani-kit-match-3/src/layout.rs`, tested): the page reads the panel rect and the HUD unit `--u` from it, so the HUD and the sprite board never disagree. Landscape when the screen is not taller than 1.05 x its width; portrait otherwise. Numbers below are for an 8x8 level.

## Desktop, 1920x1080 (landscape)

- **Margin** 32 px. **Board**: 96 px cells (the cap: the 64 px atlas shapes soften above it), 768 x 768 px, vertically centred (top at y 156). **Gap** panel to board 81 px (two margins plus half the tray rim). Panel plus gap plus board are centred horizontally as one group (panel left edge at x 326, board from x 826 to 1594).
- **HUD panel** 420 px wide (24 % of the width, clamped 200 to 420), `--u` = 30 px (width / 13, clamped 13 to 30), and only as tall as its content: 13.6 u plus 50 px = 458 px (y 155 to 613). Measured in the page (`review/shots.mjs` fit pass, level 3 with its two goal chips and the banner showing, which is the most the panel ever holds) the content needs 454 px at `--u` 30, 387 at 25.2, 364 at 23.6, 299 at 18.9, 250 at 15 and 229 at 13.3: a line of 13.45 u + 50 px, so 13.6 u + 50 px is that line with about 4 px of air and the panel is never shorter than its content (`HUD_UNITS`, `HUD_FIXED_PX`; the test `panel_is_at_least_as_tall_as_its_measured_content` holds the measured points). Without the banner the content needs 405 px at `--u` 30 and the spare room is spread evenly between the rows (no empty strip at the bottom). On smaller landscape screens the panel gets shorter and its unit shrinks with it (1366x768: 393 px, `--u` 25.2; 1280x720: 371 px, 23.6). A phone held sideways keeps the unit at 15 or more (`MIN_LANDSCAPE_UNIT`) by making Puff smaller instead (844x390: panel 254 px, `--u` 15, the content needing 250 px; Puff 65 px; 740x360 and 667x375 the same panel); a screen too short even for that (568x320) shrinks the unit further (13.3) and the panel with it, the content still fitting (231 px for 229). The level name and the MOVES and SCORE labels never go below 10 px, and on short screens they are drawn stronger. The ink outline on the moves number and the banner scales with the unit (2 px and a 5 px solid drop at `--u` 30), so it stays crisp on small text. Cream, ink border 4 px, hard drop shadow 6 x 8 px.
- **Puff** under the panel, centred on it: 190 px body, standing on his ground line at y 937 (panel and Puff are centred as one column); his reach (body, hop and star, 1.6 x his size) ends above the panel's drop shadow, so nothing of him overlaps the panel. On a short landscape screen he shrinks (down to 12 % of the height) before the panel's text does.
- Panel contents, top to bottom: level name (0.62 u) and the pause button (II, lilac) on one row; MOVES box (the number 2.3 u in yellow with ink, the label 0.55 u); SCORE box (label, score 1.15 u, star bar with three star marks that light at the thresholds: a lit mark is sun yellow, 1.18 x bigger, with a thick ink outline and a warm highlight, and stays centred on its threshold (its pop animates its scale, never its centring), so the earned marks stand out on a full bar; stars text); goal chips (piece-shaped svg icon, have/need, 0.9 u; a met goal turns green); the banner at the bottom.

## Phone, 390x844 (portrait)

- **Margin** 12 px. **HUD strip** across the top: 367 x 202 px, `--u` = 24.4 px (the strip's width / 15, or the height's share when that is smaller), the strip as tall as its content needs at that unit: 6.2 u + 50 px (measured: 150 px at `--u` 16.4, 196 at 24.4, 225 at 28.7, with the goal chips in the last row inside the border; `PORTRAIT_HUD_UNITS`, `PORTRAIT_HUD_FIXED_PX`; it used to be 23 % of the height, 182 px here, and the chips ran 14 px past its border), laid out as a grid: level name and pause on the first row; MOVES box (1.5 u) left and SCORE box (with its SCORE label, 0.45 u, the score and the star bar) right; goal chips on the third row.
- **Board** under the strip, at least 1.5 u + 6 px below it so the banner (hanging about 1.4 u under the strip) never touches a piece (`PORTRAIT_BANNER_GAP_UNITS`; on the smallest phones the board gives up about 5 % of its size for it): 42 px cells, 337 x 337 px, centred in the space between the strip and Puff.
- **Puff** centred under the board, 118 px, on the ground line at y 832, eyes looking up at the board.
- Touch targets: the board cells are 42 px (fingertip sized); menu buttons are about 42 px tall; the pause button is drawn a bit bigger in the strip (at least 17 px text, 0.85 u) and its tap target is at least 48 x 48 px on every screen (an invisible area around the drawn button; `review/shots.mjs` checks it with `elementFromPoint` on the phone and on each landscape size).

## Popups and states

- **Score texts** (white, ink outline, 3 vmin, 18 to 34 px): from each match's centre, rising about a cell, living at most until the pieces start to fall (`float_s`, capped at the pop time); texts closer than 1.6 x 0.75 cells merge into one. Never over moving pieces (geocheck popups check).
- **Combo callouts** ("Nice!" to "Unstoppable!", yellow; the biggest in pink, 5 to 6 vmin, shrunk to fit 90 % of the panel's width): off the board. Desktop: right under the panel, centred on it, above Puff (as if he shouts it). Phone: in the strip between the HUD and the board, fitted to the strip's height; the banner steps aside while a callout shows. They pop in and fade; they do not rise or shake; 0.8 s (`callout_s`).
- **Banner** (in the panel, bottom; on a phone just under the strip): "Few moves left!" (3 or fewer, pink), "Last move!", "Sugar Rush!" (yellow, pulsing).
- **Level intro card**: centred over the board for 1.4 s, "Level N: name" and the goal line; the HUD panel is already filled behind it.
- **Title screen**: the logo and menu sit where the HUD panel goes in play (hidden on the title), so the attract board stays clear and readable: in landscape the column above Puff (logo on two lines, Play, Levels and Settings stacked, and the keys hint when the column is at least 440 px tall, as at 1920x1080); in portrait the strip above the board (logo on one line, Play full width, Levels and Settings side by side, no keys hint). Everything is sized from a title unit (`--t`: landscape the smaller of width / 14 and height / 17, portrait the smaller of width / 16 and (height - 22) / 7.2, clamped 12 to 34 px) with minimum font sizes (logo 22 to 24 px, buttons 15 px). The attract board's score popups, blasts and sparkles stay hidden (its pieces still pop); Puff stays visible under the column.
- **Result card** (win or lose): a dim (40 %) over the board and its tray only, and a cream card with an ink border centred over the board's cells, at most 92 % of the board wide, so it never reaches the HUD panel: the heading (pink), stars (win), the score or what was missing, and buttons in a wrapping row (Next level on a win, Play again, Title screen). It is sized from a card unit (`--r` = the board's side / 20, clamped 10 to 40 px) with minimum font sizes (heading 22 px, stars 34 px, text 14 px, buttons 15 px), so it fits a phone held sideways (844x390: board 337 px, `--r` 16.8). The HUD panel and Puff stay bright (only the board is dimmed), lit marks and all, the pause button hidden while the result is up. The stats line keeps "Score N in M moves" and "best cascade xN" whole; a narrow card breaks only after the dot.
- **Pause**: full-screen dim with Resume, Restart level, Settings, Quit to title; the sim is frozen.
- The debug overlay is hidden in every review capture.
