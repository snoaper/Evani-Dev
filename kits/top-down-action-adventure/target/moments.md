# Signature moments

Each is a scripted capture moment (`capture.frame { moment }`, the kit's capture moments, to be listed in `apps/top-down-action-adventure/evani.toml` `[review] moments` in the art pass). Framing for every moment: the whole room is in frame with walls on all four sides, the HUD strip above it, and the hero is not under a HUD element.

## gate_hall

Card tier: Low. Must show: Wren just inside the gate hall at the start, two gloop slimes wandering, the heart pickup, the open doorway on the east wall, full HUD (3 hearts, 0 keys, 0 gems, empty item slot, a one-room map). Must not show: any text over the room, a slime already touching Wren.

## sword_hit

Card tier: Ultra. Must show: the sword arc out in front of Wren striking a gloop slime, hit sparks, the slime flashing white and pushed back a few pixels, the heart count unchanged. Must not show: the sparks hiding the slime or the hero.

## locked_door

Card tier: Low. Must show: Wren with a brass key one step from the locked cellar door, the keyhole plate readable, the key count 1 in the HUD. Must not show: the door already open.

## bolt_wand

Card tier: Ultra. Must show: the vault alcove open (the gate raised, the switch plate pressed), Wren holding the bolt wand pickup or firing a bolt at a moss-spitter, the item slot filled in the HUD. Must not show: a bolt hidden behind the HUD.

## hurt

Card tier: Low. Must show: Wren flashing after a hit from a husk, the heart count down by one, knockback visible as the hero a few pixels from the enemy, a small screen shake. Must not show: a game over.

## boss

Card tier: Ultra. Must show: the Warden at half health with its core glowing, a fan of seeds crossing the room, Wren dodging, the boss room walls with torches. Must not show: the HUD over the boss.

## dawn_shard

Card tier: Ultra. Must show: the Dawn Shard on its pedestal after the Warden has fallen, glowing, Wren walking to it, the "YOU WIN" state not yet shown (it follows the touch). Must not show: enemies still alive.
