## Mossgate Keep: the cast, in one paragraph

Wren, a short hero with a rust-red cap, a teal tunic, a brass short sword and a round wooden shield, wakes at the gate of a mossy keep. Green gloop slimes wobble around the first rooms, grey flit-bats dart about the cellar, a husk (an empty suit of armour) guards the armoury, a moss-spitter statue spits seeds in the vault, and the Warden (a tall hooded suit of armour with a glowing blue core) holds the Dawn Shard in the last room. Everything is original; no creature, item, name or tune comes from any existing game.

## What a capture is compared on

- One whole room visible at once, walls on all four sides, doors where the neighbours are.
- The hero, enemies and pickups read at a glance against the floor (distinct silhouettes and colours).
- The HUD strip shows hearts, keys, gems, the item slot and the room map, and does not cover the room.
